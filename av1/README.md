| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|

| Criterion | Points |

## How AI is used here

The statement allows AI for study, organizing ideas and review, and makes the student
responsible for the content. The final text is individual. In this repository that means:

- **AI does:** study pages per lesson, pointers to where each concept lives in the course
  material, practice questions, and review of the student's drafts against the rubric.
- **The student does:** every case answer and both variations, in their own words, and checks
  each argument against the course material before citing it.
- **Record:** every AI-assisted step goes in [`ai-usage-log.md`](ai-usage-log.md) so the final
  statement is accurate. Required wording: *"Utilizei [ferramenta] para [finalidade]. Conferi
  ou alterei [partes] com base em [materiais]."*
